GTM agents should be open-source.

Your GTM asks you to be the creative for your own customers. Nobody can do that for you — so you need complete creative control over the thing that talks to them.

A vendor's agent applies a vendor's taste to your market. You can't open it, can't change it, can't teach it what you know about your buyers.

Open source hands you the whole thing. Build on foundations that already work, then bend them to your situation. Inherit what works. Build on what's great. Tweak what's yours.

How the brain thinks.

Everything downstream rests on this, so it's the part that had to be right.

It cites and caches every source

Quote, link, date pulled. You can trace any claim back to where it came from, and it holds the source so the trail doesn't rot.

It starts at your customer's problem

Deep voice-of-customer first, not your positioning. Everything downstream traces to something a buyer actually said.

It listens to your sales calls

Granola, Gong, and the rest feed straight back in, so the brain sharpens every time you talk to someone.

It audits your company

Checks whether you could be solving a bigger problem than the one you're selling against today.

It builds the offer to be worth more

The most convincing offer is the one that hands your customer more value. It works that out before writing a word.

It finds problems, then starts fixing them

Surfaces what it found for your review first. Strategy lives in the brain, not scattered across six tools.
