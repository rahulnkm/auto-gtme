What Creates Winning Cold Email Messaging In 2025
________________


Offer Tiers:
Tier 1 → Incredible Offer (MISC Offer Unique + Extremely Valuable): 25-200 Contacts = 1 Lead
Tier 2 → Good Offer (TikTok Shop, Influencer Marketing, PMF SaaS): 200-500 Contacts = 1 Lead
Tier 3 → Decent Offer (Email Marketing, Ad Creative, Cold Email): 500-1,000 Contacts = 1 Lead
Tier 4 → Bad Offer (SEO, Cybersecurity, Recruiting): 1,000-10,000 Contacts = 1 Lead
________________


Messaging Principles
1. Sub <75 word email scripts
2. Strong frontend/introductory offer, specifically for cold traffic
3. Tailor messaging and offer according to the specific industry segment & job title persona
4. Great B2B company/founder funnel (Optimized website, social media, SEO)
5. Email messaging is written in a casual, easy to read tone
6. Great social proof that's adjacent to the industries your reaching out to
7. Offer directly solves a persona-level pain point or desire
8. Utilization of hyper-personalization with AI, if the use case makes sense
9. Use of pattern disrupts in preview text
10. Zero filler words
