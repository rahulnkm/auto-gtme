Script Writing Checklist
________________


Audit your scripts for the following
1. Email <65 words?
2. Validated/Great Offer?
3. Industry relevant wording?
4. Unique Angle?
5. Every Sentence Spintaxed?
6. Wording as relevant to List Targeting as possible?
7. Email easy to read?
8. Pattern Disrupt?
9. Zero Filler?
10. If Personalization is present, is it Meaningful?
