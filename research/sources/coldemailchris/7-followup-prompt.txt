Follow-Up Cold Email Sequence Generator Prompt
Context
I need you to create a follow-up email sequence for prospects who responded positively (expressed interest) to my original cold email campaign. These prospects have already shown initial interest but haven't yet moved forward in the sales process.
Original Campaign Context
[PASTE YOUR ORIGINAL COLD EMAIL SCRIPTS HERE - This will help maintain consistency in tone, messaging, and value proposition]
Requirements
Sequence Structure
* Number of emails: 10 scripts
* Timeline: Chronological order with 3-5 day gaps between each email
* Word limit: Maximum 75 words per email
* Audience: Prospects who responded as "interested" to the original campaign
Content Guidelines
* Value-driven: Each email must provide genuine value, not just sales pitches
* Relevant: Content should directly relate to the original campaign topic/offering
* Progressive: Build upon previous emails to create a logical sequence
* Actionable: Include clear, soft calls-to-action that move prospects forward
* Personal: Maintain a conversational, human tone
Email Sequence Framework
Please structure each email with:
1. Email #X (Day X): [Timeline indicator]
2. Subject Line: [Compelling subject line under 50 characters]
3. Body: [Email content under 75 words]
4. CTA: [Clear next step]
Sequence Strategy
* Emails 1-3: Nurture interest with valuable insights/resources
* Emails 4-6: Address common objections and provide social proof
* Emails 7-8: Create gentle urgency and showcase results
* Emails 9-10: Final value-add and soft close
