This messaging concept allows us to deploy personalized script call outs & persona-level relevance at scale with AI.


Here's how this concept works in action and why it's important:


When people ask "what makes a good cold email script?"


Everyone typically the same 2 answers:


1. Messaging/offer is relevant to their situation
2. Messaging is personalized to the prospect


Most understand this, but get both wrong.


They’ll call out the industry of the prospect’s company in the email and call that “relevance”
They’ll have AI generate first lines about their local sports team and call that “personalized”


Both of these are flawed and add no additional value to the email OR push the prospect closer to a sale.


The concept I’ve developed is the idea of “personalized relevance”

Relevant messaging that is generated from AI research plus copywriting that is tailored to each prospect’s specific persona needs and company level triggers.
